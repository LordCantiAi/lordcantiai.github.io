DeepSeek v4-flash

Production. Local ledger 558,221,824 cacheRead vs dashboard 560,076,880 — within 0.3%. Paid burn ~$3.14 across ~1,514 calls: the near-free cache does the heavy lifting. Best under stable-head reuse & retries; weakest under pure linear tail growth (each first-send tail misses once).

That is precisely why the old churning-head workload burned, and why a byte-stable head fixes it.
